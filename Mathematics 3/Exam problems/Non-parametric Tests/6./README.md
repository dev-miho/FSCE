
## Problem Statement

A virus detection algorithm, which is in the early stages of development, is being
tested to see how many attempts it takes to detect a virus on a given computer.
For this purpose, **100 computers** with the virus were taken and data was collected
on how many attempts it took the algorithm to detect the virus.

## Observed Data

| S_k | ≤2 | 3  | 4  | ≥5 |
|-----|----|----|----|----|
| N_k | 20 | 45 | 20 | 15 |

## Question

> With a significance level of **5%**, test whether the number of attempts
> until the first detection of the virus on a computer follows **G(0.3)**
> (Geometric distribution with p = 0.3).
