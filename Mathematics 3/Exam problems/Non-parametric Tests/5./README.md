## Problem Statement

In a factory, fruit candies called **"Mix"** are produced. The packaging states that
inside there are candies with **4 flavors**: pineapple, mango, strawberry, and peach
in a ratio of **8:5:4:3**, respectively.

The production controller wanted to verify if this is actually true and took a package
of **200 candies** and got the following data:

## Observed Data

| Flavor           | Pineapple | Mango | Strawberry | Peach |
|------------------|-----------|-------|------------|-------|
| Number of Candies | 92       | 46    | 42         | 20    |

## Question

> With a significance level of **5%**, can the controller claim that the distribution
> of flavors in the package is the same as the distribution stated on the packaging?
