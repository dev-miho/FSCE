## Problem Statement

A biologist wants to examine the presence of a certain bacteria in river crabs
in a river. The probability **p** that a crab is a carrier of the bacteria in the
population is **unknown**.

To estimate this probability, the biologist took **5 samples of 15 crabs** each
and registered the number of infected crabs. The biologist got the following results:

## Observed Data

| Sample                    | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---------------------------|---|---|---|---|---|
| Number of Infected Crabs  | 2 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 1 |

## Question

> Based on the data, find the estimate for the unknown parameter **p**
> using the **Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE)** method.
