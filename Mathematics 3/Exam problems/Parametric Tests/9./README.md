## Problem Statement

A french fry manufacturer wants to check the quality of production.
According to the standards, the expected mass of one package should be **750 grams**
and it comes from a **normal distribution** with a **standard deviation of 5 grams**.

For this purpose, a sample of **5 packages** was taken and their mass was measured,
giving the following results:

## Observed Data

```775 760 745 740 738```

## Question

> With a significance level of **5%**, can it be concluded that there is
> a change in the mass of french fries in one package?
