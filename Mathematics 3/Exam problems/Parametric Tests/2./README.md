According to existing data, it is considered that for an average person, the average number of steps
per day is 7200 with a standard deviation of 900. It is believed that the data is normally distributed.
A study has been conducted to examine whether the average daily number of steps for a new group of
people differs from the previously known results in terms of their daily step count.
For that purpose, a random sample of n = 25 people was taken, and their average daily steps were
measured. The sample data are as follows.

``` 5310, 6690, 7388, 6253, 5319, 7660, 7834, 8084, 6676, 6903, 7148, 7532,6426, 7655, 7220, 6370, 6671, 7097, 8829, 8748, 8692, 7237, 6041, 6575, 6252 ```

Using an appropriate test, examine whether the sample’s mean number of steps is less than the previously
known value, at a significance level α = 0.01.
