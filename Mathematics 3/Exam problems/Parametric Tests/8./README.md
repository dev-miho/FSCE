In a food laboratory, a new recipe for protein meals is being developed with the aim of reducing the variance in the amount of protein per meal. To test whether the protein content in meals prepared according to the new recipe indeed has lower variance, a sample of 10 meals prepared using the current recipe and 15 meals prepared using the new recipe is taken.

The amount of protein in the 10 meals prepared using the current recipe (in grams) is:

#### Old recipe: ``` 21, 20, 15, 19, 24, 25, 21, 19, 19,  21.```
#### New recipe: ``` 22, 26, 28, 22, 22, 25, 21, 24, 24, 23, 27, 27, 23, 25, 22 ```

At a significance level of 5%, test whether the variance of the amount of protein in a meal prepared using the new recipe is smaller than the variance of the amount of protein in a meal prepared using the current recipe.
