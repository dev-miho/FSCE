One group attends classes online, while the other attends in person. He wants to examine whether there is a difference in student performance between the two groups.

Therefore, he randomly selected 11 students from the in-person group and 10 students from the online group and recorded their final exam scores:
#### In-person attendance:``` 82, 75, 88, 79, 81, 85, 77, 80, 83, 78, 79 ```
#### Online:``` 79, 81, 76, 84, 80, 78, 82, 77, 83, 79 ```

Is it possible, at a significance level of 0.05, to conclude that the expected results of students in the two groups differ?

It is assumed that the final exam scores in both groups follow a normal distribution with equal variances.
