An inspection service collected data from a beverage production factory that sells its product in a single package size. The data were randomly selected after a change in the packaging machine, due to suspicion that the standard deviation of the filled volume is greater than before. The company claims that the standard deviation is 2.

The collected data are as follows (in ml):

``` 743, 751, 748, 757, 752, 745, 750, 755, 749, 754, 742, 756, 744, 753, 746 ```

It is known that the volume of the beverage in the packages follows a normal distribution. Using a significance level of 5%, test whether the standard deviation of the beverage volume in the packages is greater than 2.
