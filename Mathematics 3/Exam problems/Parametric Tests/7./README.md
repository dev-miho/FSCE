A food laboratory is developing a new recipe for protein meals with the goal of increasing the expected amount of protein per meal. To determine whether meals prepared according to the new recipe actually contain a higher amount of protein, a sample of 10 meals prepared according to the current recipe and a sample of 15 meals prepared according to the new recipe were taken.

The protein content in the 10 meals prepared according to the current recipe (expressed in grams) is:

#### Old recipe: ``` 21, 20, 15, 19, 24, 25, 21, 19, 19,  21.```
#### New recipe: ``` 22, 26, 28, 22, 22, 25, 21, 24, 24, 23, 27, 27, 23, 25, 22 ```

It is assumed that the protein content per meal for both recipes follows a normal distribution with equal variances.

Using a significance level of 5%, test whether the expected amount of protein in a meal prepared according to the new recipe is greater than the expected amount of protein in a meal prepared according to the current recipe.
