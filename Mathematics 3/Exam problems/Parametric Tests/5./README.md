The analytics team of a chain of jewelry stores that buy gold randomly selected 35 different weeks and wants to analyze whether the mass (in mg) of gold purchased in a week by the jewelry chain has increased after a marketing campaign conducted during these weeks.

It is known that the mass of purchased gold follows a normal distribution. The results are as follows:

``` 120, 125, 130, 110, 115, 140, 135, 128, 122, 118, 132, 140, 138, 121, 133, 130, 126, 127, 129, 119, 136, 125, 123, 131, 124, 137, 139, 120, 128, 132, 125, 100, 101, 102, 103 ```

The expected mass of gold purchased in a week before the marketing campaign was 95 mg. Using the collected data and a significance level of 0.05, test whether the expected weekly mass of purchased gold has increased as a result of the marketing campaign.
